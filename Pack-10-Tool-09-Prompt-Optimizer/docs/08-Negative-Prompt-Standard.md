# 08 — Negative Prompt Standard

Negative rules are product-aware and evidence-based.

Good:
- no hand contact with neckline;
- no digital zoom;
- no new people;
- no product color change.

Bad generic:
- no hats, when a real hat exists;
- no strings, when a real drawstring exists;
- no accessories, when accessories are part of ProductDNA.

Do not invent a separate runtime `negativePrompt` field unless capability proves it exists.
